Questions

Good to know

How long does it take?

The Song and The Duet are ready within 24 hours, usually sooner. Rush is ready the same day. You can watch your place in line on your order page.

Do I get to keep it?

Yes. Download the MP3 and it's yours forever. Your private link stays open for 60 days so you have plenty of time to save it.

Is this made with AI?

Yes. The music and voices are made with AI tools running on our own studio computer. A person reads your story, checks the words and only sends the best take.

Can I use it at my wedding or post it online?

Yes. Play it anywhere, post it, share it. It's your song.

What if a name is sung wrong?

Reply to your song email and we'll redo it once for free. Spelling a tricky name the way it sounds helps (for example "Pawlee").

Is my story private?

Yes. Your song lives on a private link only you get. We never post customer songs without asking.
